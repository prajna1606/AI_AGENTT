# SRVCABLE: Research Brief (as of 23 Sep 2026)

## 1. Snapshot  
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires and conductors for utilities and data‑centre developers. In Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 Jun 2026) revenue was **₹1,248 cr**, up **18.0 % YoY**; EBITDA (earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation) was **₹140 cr**, giving an **EBITDA margin** (EBITDA as a % of revenue) of **11.2 %** (down 170 bps) and PAT (profit after tax) **₹82 cr** (+5.9 % YoY) [D3]. The order book – value of contracts yet to be fulfilled – stood at **₹3,900 cr** as of 30 Jun 2026, up from ₹3,150 cr a year earlier [D3]. Receivable days rose to **96 days** from 78 days, indicating slower cash collection [D2]. Net‑debt‑to‑equity is **0.35 ×**, and Bharuch plant capex totals **₹620 cr**, with ~70 % already spent [D2].

## 2. Bull case  
- **Revenue momentum and large order backlog:** Q1 revenue grew 18 % and the order book expanded 24 % YoY, providing a solid pipeline for the rest of FY27 [D3].  
- **Capacity boost and data‑centre opportunity:** The new Bharuch plant adds ~30 % cable capacity and is slated for commercial production in Q3 FY27; data‑centre cables, currently ~9 % of domestic revenue, are targeted to double in two years [D2].  
- **Balanced leverage and export upside:** Net‑debt‑to‑equity of 0.35 × suggests manageable debt, while exports now represent 21 % of revenue, up from 16 % YoY, indicating diversification and growth potential [D3].

## 3. Bear case  
- **Margin pressure from copper costs:** EBITDA margin fell 170 bps to 11.2 % as copper prices rose ~14 % in the quarter, eroding profitability [D1][D3].  
- **Deteriorating working‑capital collection:** Receivable days increased to 96 days, raising cash‑flow risk and indicating reliance on a few large utility customers [D2].  
- **Potential tax liability:** The company disclosed a GST demand order of **₹46.3 cr** (including interest and penalty) for FY 2020‑21/22, which could affect cash resources if not resolved [D7].

## 4. Open questions  
- **Q1 revenue figure:** D1 reports revenue of **₹1,428 cr** (+35 % YoY) versus D3’s **₹1,248 cr** (+18 % YoY) for the same quarter [D1][D3]. Which is correct?  
- **Promoter pledge level:** D4 (2024) states a 35 % pledge of promoter holding, while D8 (2026) shows only **4.1 %** of promoter shares pledged (≈2.4 % of total) [D4][D8]. Has the pledge dramatically fallen, or is the older figure outdated?  
- **Impact of GST demand:** No follow‑up on the outcome of the ₹46.3 cr GST order; will the appeal succeed and what is the realistic financial impact? [D7]  
- **Cash‑flow details:** The filings do not disclose free cash flow or debt service coverage; investors need clarity on liquidity given higher receivable days.  
- **Segment revenue breakdown:** No quantitative split for data‑centre versus traditional power‑cable revenue; the 9 % share is a target, not a current figure.
