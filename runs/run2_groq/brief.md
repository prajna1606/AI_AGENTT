# SRVCABLE: Research Brief (as of 23 Sep 2026)

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd makes power cables, wires and conductors for utilities and data‑centre developers【D3】. For Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 Jun 2026) the company filed revenue of **₹1,248 crore** (up 18.0 % YoY) and EBITDA of **₹140 crore**, giving an **EBITDA margin** (EBITDA ÷ revenue) of **11.2 %**【D3】. Profit after tax was **₹82 crore**【D3】. The **order book** (contracts awaiting fulfilment) stood at **₹3,900 crore**【D3】. **Receivable days** (average time to collect sales) rose to **96** from 78 a year ago【D2】, and **net‑debt‑to‑equity** (net debt ÷ shareholders’ equity) was **0.35 ×**【D2】. The share closed at **₹1,012** on the NSE【D1】.

## 2. Bull case
- **Revenue growth and strong order book:** Q1 revenue grew 18 % YoY and the order book expanded to ₹3,900 crore, indicating a solid pipeline【D3】.  
- **Capacity expansion and data‑centre focus:** The new Bharuch plant adds about 30 % capacity【D3】; about 70 % of the ₹620 crore capex is already spent【D2】. Data‑centre cables now represent ~9 % of domestic sales【D2】.  
- **Export contribution:** Exports made up 21 % of revenue, up from 16 % a year earlier, providing geographic diversification【D3】.

## 3. Bear case
- **Margin pressure:** EBITDA margin fell to 11.2 % from 12.9 % (‑170 bps) as copper prices rose about 14 % in the quarter, squeezing profitability【D3】.  
- **Slower cash conversion:** Receivable days increased to 96, reflecting delayed payments from large utility customers and raising cash‑flow risk【D2】.  
- **Regulatory and penalty uncertainties:** The company disclosed a GST demand of **₹46.3 crore** but expects no material impact【D7】. A separate local operator, Sarvottam Cable Network, was fined **₹8 lakh** for illegal overhead wiring; it is unclear whether this liability affects the listed company【D6】.
