# SRVCABLE: Research Brief (as as of 23 Sep 2026)

## 1. Snapshot  
Sarvottam Cables Ltd makes power cables, wires and conductors for utilities and data‑centre developers. In Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 Jun 2026) revenue was **₹1,248 cr**, up **18.0 % YoY**; EBITDA (earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation) was **₹140 cr**, giving an **EBITDA margin** (EBITDA as a % of revenue) of **11.2 %** (down 170 bps) and PAT (profit after tax) **₹82 cr** (+5.9 % YoY) [D3]. The **order book** (value of contracts yet to be fulfilled) stood at **₹3,900 cr**, up from ₹3,150 cr a year earlier [D3]. Receivable days (average collection period) rose to **96 days** from 78 days [D2]. Net‑debt‑to‑equity (ratio of net debt to shareholders’ equity) is **0.35 ×** [D2]. Bharuch plant capex totals **₹620 cr**, with about **70 %** already spent [D2].

## 2. Bull case  
- **Revenue growth and strong order backlog:** Q1 revenue rose 18 % YoY and the order book expanded ~24 % YoY, providing a solid pipeline for FY27 [D3].  
- **Capacity expansion and data‑centre upside:** The new Bharuch plant adds roughly 30 % cable capacity and is slated for commercial production in Q3 FY27; data‑centre cables now account for about 9 % of domestic revenue and the company aims to double this share in two years [D2].  
- **Moderate leverage and export diversification:** Net‑debt‑to‑equity of 0.35 × indicates manageable debt, while exports now represent 21 % of revenue, up from 16 % YoY [D3].

## 3. Bear case  
- **Margin pressure from higher copper costs:** EBITDA margin fell 170 bps to 11.2 % as copper prices rose roughly 14 % during the quarter [D1][D3].  
- **Slower cash collection:** Receivable days increased to 96 days, raising cash‑flow risk and reflecting reliance on a few large utility customers [D2].  
- **Potential GST liability:** The company disclosed a GST demand order of **₹46.3 cr** (including interest and penalty) for FY 2020‑21/22; while management expects no material impact, the amount could affect cash resources if the appeal fails [D7].

## 4. Open questions  
- **Revenue figure discrepancy:** D1 reports revenue of **₹1,428 cr** (+35 % YoY) for Q1 FY27, whereas D3 shows **₹1,248 cr** (+18 % YoY) [D1][D3]. Which number is correct?  
- **Promoter pledge level:** D4 (2024) states a 35 % pledge of promoter holding, while D8 (June 2026) shows only **4.1 %** of promoter shares pledged (≈2.4 % of total) [D4][D8]. Has the pledge fallen sharply, or is the older figure outdated?  
- **Data‑centre revenue share:** The 9 % figure is described as current domestic revenue share, but no quantitative breakdown is provided; is this share higher in Q1 FY27?  
- **Liquidity metrics:** Free cash flow and debt‑service coverage are not disclosed; how robust is liquidity given higher receivable days?  
- **Impact of GST demand:** No update on the outcome of the GST appeal; what is the realistic financial impact if the demand is upheld?
