# SRVCABLE: Research Brief (as of 23 Sep 2026)

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires and conductors for utilities and data‑centre developers. For Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 Jun 2026) the company reported revenue of **₹1,248 crore** (up 18.0 % YoY) and EBITDA of **₹140 crore**, giving an **EBITDA margin** (EBITDA ÷ revenue) of **11.2 %**【D3】. Profit after tax was **₹82 crore**【D3】. The order book – contracts awaiting fulfilment – stood at **₹3,900 crore**【D3】. Receivable days (average time to collect sales) rose to **96** from 78 a year ago【D2】, while net‑debt‑to‑equity (net debt ÷ shareholders’ equity) was **0.35 ×**【D2】. The share closed at **₹1,012** on the NSE【D1】.

## 2. Bull case
- **Revenue momentum and deep order book:** Q1 revenue grew 18 % YoY and the order book expanded to ₹3,900 crore, indicating strong pipeline strength【D3】.  
- **Capacity and product diversification:** The new Bharuch plant adds ~30 % capacity and is slated for commercial production in Q3 FY27, with 70 % of the ₹620 crore capex already spent【D2】【D3】. Data‑centre cables now represent ~9 % of domestic sales and the company aims to double this share in two years【D2】.  
- **Export growth:** Export sales rose to 21 % of total revenue, up from 16 % a year earlier, providing geographic diversification【D3】.

## 3. Bear case
- **Margin compression:** EBITDA margin fell to 11.2 % from 12.9 % (‑170 bps) as copper prices jumped ~14 % in the quarter, squeezing profitability【D1】【D3】.  
- **Slowing cash conversion:** Receivable days increased to 96, reflecting slower payments from large utility customers and raising cash‑flow risk【D2】.  
- **Regulatory and tax headwinds:** The firm faces a GST demand of **₹46.3 crore** (including interest/penalty) and an **₹8 lakh** penalty for illegal overhead wiring by a related operator, which could affect finances despite management’s view of no material impact【D7】【D6】.

## 4. Open questions
- **Revenue figure conflict:** D1 reports Q1 revenue of **₹1,428 crore**, while D3 states **₹1,248 crore** for the same period. Which number reflects the audited result?【D1】【D3】  
- **GST demand impact:** The company says the ₹46.3 crore GST order will not materially affect its position, but details on cash impact and timing are missing【D7】.  
- **Penalty relevance:** The ₹8 lakh fine concerns “Sarvottam Cable Network,” a local TV/broadband operator. Is this entity part of the listed company or a separate affiliate?【D6】  
- **Promoter pledge trajectory:** D4 (2024) notes a 35 % promoter pledge, whereas D8 (Jun 2026) shows only 4.1 % of promoter holdings pledged. What is the current absolute pledged percentage and the reason for the change?【D4】【D8】  
- **Future growth guidance feasibility:** Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15‑17 %【D2]; given margin pressure and higher receivable days, how realistic is this target?【D2】
